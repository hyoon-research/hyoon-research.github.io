I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the **University of California, Berkeley**, working with Prof. Lisa Alvarez-Cohen and Prof. David Sedlak. I earned my Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering from **Cornell University**.

My research sits at the intersection of **environmental geochemistry, interfacial biogeochemistry, environmental microbiology, and data-driven modeling**.

---

### Research Interests
- **Environmental & Aqueous Geochemistry:** Mineral-water interfacial dynamics, speciation.
- **Predictive Environmental Modeling:** Integration of machine learning (XGBoost, GNNs) with domain knowledge.
